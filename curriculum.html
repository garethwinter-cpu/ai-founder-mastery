Seventeen lessons. One company, built department by department.

Every lesson builds one department of the business, in the order a business needs its parts, and installs the AI team that keeps it running. Tuesday, the lesson: the finished thing shown first, then the idea behind it. Thursday, the lab: the system installed in your business, live, in three timed builds. Each week ends with something running, and by Demo Day those pieces run as one: your Founder Operating System. Teachers are mapped once the curriculum is settled.
