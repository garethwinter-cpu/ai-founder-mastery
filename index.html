AI for Founders Mastery

AI Mastery goes deep into AI. AI for Founders goes deep into the business. Seventeen lessons and a graduation in which a founder builds the company one department at a time, storefront to cockpit, each with an AI team installed and running. AI is the means, not the subject. You leave as the orchestrator of a company that does more with a leaner team, with your Founder Operating System installed and running: the company brain, the storefront, funnel and traffic teams, the sales, operations and finance teams, and the founder’s cockpit.

Who it is for

Two founders in the room

From the AI for Founders quiz and the AI Mastery buyer surveys. Both have something real to sell. Both have growth capped by their own hours. They feel it differently, and the two tracks in every lab are built on that difference.

The solo founder

"Your clients book you, not your business."

Coaches, consultants, practitioners and creators who are the business. Six in ten quiz takers. Content and social media eat a quarter of their week, then marketing, then leads and follow-up. They start with marketing, and the storefront in weeks 1 to 4 is where the week changes first.

The founder with a team of up to ten

"Decisions and complaints always go through me, and that wears me down."

Consulting and professional services lead. One in five quiz takers, and they buy at nearly twice the solo rate. Email, admin and approvals eat their week. They start with operations, and the sorted inbox in week 6 is the first system that runs without them.
